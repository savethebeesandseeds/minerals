# Waajacu's Minerals: the open mineral engine

Manufacturing cannot become broadly capable while knowledge and access to
minerals remain fragmented. A mineral may be described by one scientific
database, sold under another name by dozens of providers, accompanied by
incomparable specifications, and represented by images whose rights or identity
cannot be established. Waajacu's Minerals exists to join those broken layers
without blurring their meaning.

The product is intended to answer three progressively harder questions:

1. **What is this mineral?** Search mineral species by name, formula, synonym,
   or external identifier.
2. **What do we actually know about it?** Show claims with provenance,
   confidence, review status, conflicts, conditions, and licenses.
3. **How can it be obtained?** Search a network of providers, compare current
   offers, expose missing information, and help a person source the right
   specification.

The engine must be useful even when the answer is incomplete. "Unknown",
"provider claim", and "generated lead" are legitimate states. Invented
certainty is not.

## Principles

- **Truth has provenance.** A model, provider, editor, and published source are
  different origins. The system retains that distinction.
- **Claims are not records.** Conflicting measurements can coexist. A preferred
  value is a reviewed selection, not a destructive overwrite.
- **Commerce never ranks science.** Availability and sponsorship do not improve
  a mineral's knowledge-search relevance.
- **Provider specifications remain provider claims.** A product match does not
  prove purity, grade, origin, availability, or business quality.
- **Synthetic images are illustrations.** They must be visibly labeled and
  retain generator, model/version, prompt hash, seed, references, and license
  metadata. They are never specimen evidence or identification ground truth.
- **Open where rights permit.** Every imported source carries license and rights
  metadata. Public exports include only data that can legally be redistributed.
- **Reproducibility over magic.** Ingestion, ranking, translation, derivation,
  and generation runs are versioned and auditable.
- **Humans retain purchasing authority.** An agent may research, compare, and
  prepare a transaction. It must not place an order without explicit approval
  of material, specification, quantity, provider, destination, and total cost.

## Product horizons

### Registry

A global identity layer for mineral species, with names, formulas, external
identifiers, properties, safety information, evidence, and media. Chemical
compounds are intentionally outside the present product scope so the mineral
corpus can become complete and dependable first. Completeness is measured by
stable identities and sourced records, not photographs; the first global
baseline can and should ship without images.

### Research engine

Cited, reproducible mineral briefs that show which claims and sources were
used. Conflicts and uncertainty should be visible rather than averaged away.

### Provider network

Provider adapters ingest permitted catalogs and APIs into normalized listings
and time-stamped offers. Searches rank eligibility and match quality before
price, while returning all unknown fields.

### Sourcing agent

When cached offers are absent, an asynchronous agent searches approved provider
connectors, records every query and result, and returns options for review. RFQs
and eventual purchases are explicit audited actions.

### Open corpus and synthetic media

Licensed datasets and curator-reviewed records form the core corpus. Synthetic
images can fill visual gaps only after the media provenance and labeling system
is complete. They supplement access; they do not counterfeit specimens.

## The promise

Waajacu's Minerals should become infrastructure: reliable enough to support a
manufacturing decision, transparent enough to challenge, and free enough that
builders across the world can use and improve it.
